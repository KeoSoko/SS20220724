# Architecture Impact Assessment – Convert Workspaces from Shared Data to Billing Groups Only

We have discovered a potential product requirement mismatch.

Current implementation:

Workspace = Shared Data Container

Meaning:

* Workspace members share receipts
* Workspace members share clients
* Workspace members share invoices
* Workspace members share quotations
* Workspace members share reports
* Workspace members share categories and learning rules (where applicable)

However, customer feedback indicates the actual requirement is:

Workspace = Billing Group

Meaning:

* One company pays for multiple users
* Each user retains their own private account and private data
* No receipt sharing
* No client sharing
* No invoice sharing
* No report sharing
* Billing only is centralized

This is a READ-ONLY impact assessment.

DO NOT change code.
DO NOT modify the database.
DO NOT create migrations.
DO NOT implement anything.
