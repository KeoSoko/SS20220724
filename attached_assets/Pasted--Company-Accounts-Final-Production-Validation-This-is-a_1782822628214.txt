# Company Accounts – Final Production Validation

This is a validation task only.

Do not redesign the architecture.
Do not add new features.
Do not refactor unless a defect is found.

The recent fixes fundamentally changed Company Accounts:

* Personal Workspace owns all user data.
* Company Account owns billing.
* workspace_members links users to the billing workspace.
* Accepting an invite must NEVER move a user's data.
* user.workspaceId now represents the user's own workspace only.
* Billing inheritance is determined through workspace_members.

## Objective

Perform a complete end-to-end audit to prove this architecture is correct.

---

## Validate these scenarios

### Scenario 1 – Existing user joins a Company Account

Create or simulate:

Owner

* Own workspace
* Active Team subscription

Member

* Existing account
* Existing receipts
* Existing invoices
* Existing clients

Accept invitation.

Verify:

✓ user.workspaceId is unchanged

✓ workspace_members row created

✓ Member keeps all receipts

✓ Member keeps all invoices

✓ Member keeps all clients

✓ Member keeps all quotations

✓ Member keeps tax reports

✓ Member inherits billing correctly

✓ Company Account page displays correctly

---

### Scenario 2 – New user joins Company Account

Invite a brand-new account.

Verify:

✓ Personal workspace created

✓ Billing membership created

✓ Access inherited

✓ No data migration occurs

---

### Scenario 3 – Remove member

Remove member from Company Account.

Verify:

✓ workspace_members removed

✓ Personal workspace remains

✓ Receipts remain

✓ Clients remain

✓ Invoices remain

✓ Billing access removed

---

### Scenario 4 – Owner subscription changes

Verify:

Owner active
→ Members active

Owner expired
→ Members expired

Owner renews
→ Members regain access

---

### Scenario 5 – Privacy audit

Confirm members CANNOT access another member's:

* receipts
* receipt images
* invoices
* quotations
* clients
* tax reports
* analytics
* recurring expenses
* email documents

Confirm merchant_category_rules remain intentionally shared.

---

### Scenario 6 – Company Account UI

Verify:

Owner sees:

* Billing Information
* Seat usage
* Invite members
* Upgrade plan

Member sees:

* Company information
* Access included
* Private data notice

Member cannot:

* Upgrade
* Cancel billing
* Manage company billing

---

### Scenario 7 – Database integrity

Verify:

user.workspaceId always points to the user's own workspace.

workspace_members contains billing relationships only.

No route changes user.workspaceId during invitation acceptance.

No route migrates receipts, invoices, quotations or clients between workspaces.
