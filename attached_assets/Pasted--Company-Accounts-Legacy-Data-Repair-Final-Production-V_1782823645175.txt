This is a controlled production repair.

====================================================
BACKGROUND
====================================================

The correct architecture is:

Personal Workspace
- owns all user data
- receipts
- invoices
- quotations
- clients
- tax reports

Company Account
- owns billing
- subscription
- seats

workspace_members
- links a personal workspace to a company billing workspace
- NEVER owns data

Invariant:

user.workspaceId MUST ALWAYS equal the user's OWN workspace.

Joining a Company Account MUST NEVER change user.workspaceId.
