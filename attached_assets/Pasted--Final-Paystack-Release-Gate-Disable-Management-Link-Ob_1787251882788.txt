# Final Paystack Release Gate — Disable Management Link + Observe Reconciliation

Implement the final two release requirements only.

Do not redesign the Paystack architecture.

Do not deploy.

Do not charge customers.

Do not mutate production subscriptions.

Do not cancel, refund, or back-charge anyone.

---

# 1. Add explicit management-link feature gate

Add:

`PAYSTACK_SUBSCRIPTION_MANAGEMENT_LINK_ENABLED`

Required behavior:

* Missing environment variable = disabled.
* `false` = disabled.
* Only explicit `true` enables the hosted Paystack management-link feature.

The default must be safe.

---

# 2. Gate the server route

Apply the feature gate to:

`/api/billing/paystack/subscription/manage-link`

When disabled:

* return a controlled response;
* do not call Paystack;
* do not generate a management link;
* do not create checkout;
* do not charge an authorization;
* do not create a subscription;
* do not cancel anything;
* do not issue refunds;
* do not collect historical missed payments.

Use an appropriate status such as `503` or existing controlled feature-unavailable response.

Include a stable machine-readable code such as:

`paystack_management_link_disabled`

Do not expose internal configuration details unnecessarily.

---

# 3. Gate server-derived eligibility

When the management-link feature is disabled:

`getPaystackRenewalStatus()`

must not tell the frontend that hosted management is available.

Specifically:

`managementLinkEligible = false`

or equivalent.

The frontend must never be able to infer or construct the provider management flow on its own.

---

# 4. Remove/hide customer CTAs while disabled

Hide both existing:

`Update payment method`

CTAs when the feature is disabled.

For:

`payment_method_needs_attention`

show controlled support messaging instead:

> Your payment method needs attention. Please contact support while we confirm the safest way to update your automatic renewal.

Do not show:

* generic Subscribe;
* Restore automatic renewal;
* recovery checkout;

merely because the management-link feature is disabled.

Existing recovery checkout may remain available only in the separate state where complete provider inspection proves that no viable Paystack subscription exists.

---

# 5. Preserve prospective-only billing policy

Explicitly preserve this invariant:

> Simple Slips will not automatically collect historical missed subscription payments discovered during billing reconciliation or recovery. Recovery establishes correct billing prospectively from the appropriate next billing date.

The management-link-disabled state must not introduce any alternate catch-up path.

No historical aggregation.

No arrears collection.

No stored authorization charging.
