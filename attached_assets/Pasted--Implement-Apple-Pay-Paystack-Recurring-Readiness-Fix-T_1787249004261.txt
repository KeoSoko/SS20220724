# Implement Apple Pay / Paystack Recurring-Readiness Fix

The Apple Pay / Paystack recurring-readiness audit is approved for implementation.

Proceed in Build mode.

Do not deploy or publish.

Do not modify existing production customer records.

Do not automatically cancel duplicate Paystack subscriptions.

Do not refund customers.

Do not charge stored authorizations.

Do not introduce a legacy one-click charge mechanism.

Preserve all Task #41, #42 and #46 Paystack protections.

---

# 1. Core model

Implement the distinction established by the audit:

**Payment successful ≠ automatic renewal ready**

A successful verified Paystack transaction may activate the customer's paid entitlement according to the existing settlement rules.

It must not automatically establish that unattended recurring billing is safely configured.

Recurring readiness must be derived from authoritative provider evidence.

---

# 2. Recurring-readiness requirements

A recurring relationship may be considered ready only when authoritative provider evidence establishes:

* successful transaction where relevant;
* correct effective billing owner;
* correct Paystack customer;
* correct Simple Slips plan;
* correct Paystack plan;
* exact provider `SUB_*`;
* subscription belongs to the expected customer;
* subscription belongs to the expected plan;
* associated provider authorization exists;
* `authorization.reusable === true` exactly;
* authorization belongs to the selected subscription relationship.

Do not use truthiness for the reusable flag.

Do not infer recurring readiness merely from:

* successful payment;
* existence of `AUTH_*`;
* existence of `SUB_*`;
* metadata;
* plan code;
* Apple Pay;
* Card;
* locally stored payment method.

---

# 3. Preserve normalized provider evidence

For newly verified Paystack transactions/relationships, safely persist the provider evidence required to understand future billing behaviour.

At minimum preserve where authoritative and available:

* Paystack transaction reference;
* Paystack transaction ID;
* transaction channel;
* customer code;
* plan code;
* subscription code;
* authorization code;
* authorization channel;
* authorization signature where appropriate;
* authorization reusable flag;
* provider verification timestamp.

Do not store sensitive card information.

Do not rewrite historical provider facts that cannot be established.

For legacy rows where these values are unavailable:

**unknown must remain unknown.**

Do not infer `card` or Apple Pay.

---

# 4. Fix payment-method recording

Remove the current behaviour that records every successful Paystack settlement as:

`payment_method = card`

when the authoritative provider channel is something else.

For new transactions, record the verified provider channel accurately.

Do not mass-rewrite historical production rows.

Historical records that cannot be proven should remain unchanged or explicitly unknown according to the safest schema design.

---

# 5. Entitlement settlement

Preserve the existing secure payment-settlement path.

If an initial payment is verified successfully but recurring readiness is false or unknown:

* record the successful payment exactly once;
* activate/extend paid entitlement according to existing rules;
* preserve payment ledger idempotency;
* do not label the payment failed;
* do not claim automatic renewal is active;
* do not create another checkout automatically.

The customer may therefore legitimately have:

`paid entitlement = active`

while:

`automatic renewal = needs setup`

These states must coexist safely.

---

# 6. Existing trusted subscription + reusable authorization

If the billing owner has:

* exact trusted active `SUB_*`;
* verified provider customer/plan ownership;
* verified reusable authorization;

then:

`automatic renewal = active`

Continue normal renewal/reconciliation behaviour.

No customer action required.

---

# 7. Existing subscription + payment method needs attention

If an exact trusted `SUB_*` exists and belongs unambiguously to the billing owner and plan, but its authorization/payment method requires customer action:

Do NOT create another subscription checkout.

Instead use Paystack's hosted subscription-management mechanism.

Implement an owner-scoped endpoint conceptually equivalent to:

`POST /api/billing/paystack/subscription/manage-link`

Before requesting the hosted link:

1. Resolve effective billing owner.
2. Acquire the appropriate billing-owner protection.
3. Reject covered workspace members from personal billing management.
4. Re-read local trusted identity.
5. Verify the provider subscription exists.
6. Verify exact customer ownership.
7. Verify exact plan ownership.
8. Verify the subscription is still appropriate for management.
9. Only then request the hosted management link from Paystack.

Return the hosted link only to the authorized billing owner.

Do not log the full management URL.

Do not persist it longer than necessary.

Do not assume an SDK wrapper exists. Verify the installed Paystack integration/API contract.

---

# 8. Missing local identity + one provider subscription

If there is no trusted local `SUB_*`, but complete authoritative provider inspection finds exactly one viable subscription for:

`billing owner + customer + plan`

then:

* recover the identity using the existing Task #46 guarded identity-recovery path;
* do not charge;
* do not create another subscription;
* inspect recurring readiness;
* if authorization requires attention, offer the hosted management link for that recovered subscription.

Identity recovery itself must not extend entitlement.

---

# 9. Multiple provider subscriptions

If complete provider inspection finds more than one viable `SUB_*` for the same billing owner/customer/plan:

**FAIL CLOSED.**

Do not:

* select the newest;
* select the oldest;
* select the subscription matching the local billing date;
* select the subscription with the latest successful payment;
* automatically cancel either subscription;
* recover either identity;
* generate a hosted management link;
* create another checkout.

Return:

`manual_review_required`

or the existing equivalent state.

The customer must not be exposed to another subscription-creation opportunity.

This behaviour must remain true under concurrent requests.

---

# 11. No provider subscription exists

Only after a **complete authoritative provider listing** proves there is no viable existing subscription for the billing owner/customer/plan may Task #46's hardened recovery checkout become available.

Preserve:

* server-owned reference;
* immutable terms;
* one-pending-owner unique constraint;
* billing-owner lock;
* provider re-check;
* checkout idempotency;
* workspace billing ownership.

Provider lookup failure or incomplete pagination must never be interpreted as:

`no subscriptions exist`

It must result in:

`reconciling`

or equivalent fail-closed state.

---

# 12. Customer-facing states

Implement clear server-derived states.

## Automatic renewal active

Meaning:

Trusted recurring relationship and provider authorization are ready.

No CTA.

## Payment method needs attention

Meaning:

Existing trusted subscription remains the canonical relationship but Paystack requires customer action for future renewal.

Suggested message:

> We couldn't complete your automatic renewal. Update your payment method securely with Paystack.

CTA:

`Update payment method`

This must use the hosted management link for the existing `SUB_*`.

## Automatic renewal needs setup

Meaning:

Payment/entitlement may be valid, but unattended renewal has not been safely established.

Do not claim the customer's payment failed.

CTA must depend on provider inspection.

Do not blindly open checkout.

## Manual review required

Meaning:

Provider relationship is ambiguous, including multiple viable subscriptions.

Do not expose checkout.

Use neutral support wording.

## Reconciling

Provider information cannot currently be established conclusively.

Do not expose checkout.

---

# 13. Do not hardcode Apple Pay as unsupported

Do not implement:

`if channel === apple_pay => recurring not supported`

unless authoritative Paystack behaviour requires that.

The application should use provider evidence.

If Paystack returns a valid exact subscription relationship with:

`authorization.reusable === true`

then evaluate it normally regardless of initial payment channel.

If Paystack returns:

`authorization.reusable === false`

or does not provide sufficient authorization evidence:

automatic renewal is not ready.
