# Replace Section 5 — Team / Company Access Policy

Do not assume that the historical `owner`, `editor`, and `viewer` workspace roles are still the authoritative Simple Slips product-access model.

Before implementing Bank Statement permissions, inspect the current Simple Slips Team / Company account architecture and determine:

* how Team / Company accounts are represented,
* how users are attached to a Team / Company account,
* whether `workspace_members` is still authoritative,
* whether `owner`, `editor`, and `viewer` are actively used,
* whether newer team/account roles or permissions have replaced them,
* how the active company/workspace is selected,
* who is permitted to upload, edit, view, and export shared business data elsewhere in the current app,
* whether billing ownership is separate from operational permissions.

Use the **current Simple Slips Team / Company permission model** as the source of truth.

Do not create a new role system specifically for Bank Statements.

Bank Statement access should follow the same permission conventions already used by the current Team / Company product wherever practical.

At minimum, define and document which existing users may:

* upload/import a statement,
* view imported statements,
* download exports,
* edit or categorise transactions in future jobs.

If the current Team / Company implementation does not yet contain sufficiently granular permissions, use the safest existing access rule and document the limitation rather than inventing a parallel permission model.

The authenticated handoff must carry only server-validated access context derived from the current Team / Company model.

Do not trust role or company/workspace information supplied by the browser.

Before implementation, report:

1. the current Team / Company data model,
2. whether `workspace_members` is still actively authoritative,
3. the current role/permission values,
4. how other shared Team / Company features enforce access,
5. the Bank Statement permission mapping you propose to reuse.

Only implement after establishing the current source of truth from the codebase.
