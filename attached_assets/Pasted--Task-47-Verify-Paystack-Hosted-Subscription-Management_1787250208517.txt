Task #47 is the final dependency before deploying the recurring-readiness release.

The purpose of this task is to verify that the hosted subscription-management-link integration used by Simple Slips matches Paystack's actual API contract, fails safely if it cannot be obtained, and preserves the prospective-only billing policy defined below.

---

# CRITICAL BILLING POLICY — NO HISTORICAL BACK-CHARGING

This is now an explicit Simple Slips billing invariant:

> **Simple Slips will not automatically collect historical missed subscription payments discovered during billing reconciliation or recovery. Recovery establishes correct billing prospectively from the appropriate next billing date.**

This applies to:

* legacy subscriptions;
* Apple Pay-originated subscription problems;
* missing `SUB_*` identities;
* failed or abandoned historical renewals;
* reconciliation discoveries;
* payment-method recovery;
* subscription-identity recovery;
* duplicate-provider-subscription remediation;
* customers affected by previous Simple Slips billing defects.

A customer must never suddenly receive multiple charges because Simple Slips discovers that one or more historical subscription renewals were not collected.

---

# 1. Historical missed payments

When reconciliation/recovery discovers historical periods for which no successful Paystack settlement exists:

Do NOT:

* charge the customer's stored authorization;
* create catch-up transactions;
* create one transaction covering accumulated arrears;
* create multiple transactions for missed months;
* create retrospective checkout attempts;
* automatically shorten entitlement to compensate;
* automatically create historical invoices intended for collection;
* move the next billing date backwards to recover missed periods.

Historical missed collections are not automatically recoverable subscription debt.

Preserve the historical record accurately, but do not automatically collect it.

---

# 2. Prospective recovery

Recovery must establish a safe billing relationship **going forward**.

Once the canonical Paystack subscription/payment relationship has been established or repaired, determine the appropriate prospective next billing date according to the legitimate current subscription relationship.

Do not derive a future charge amount by multiplying:

`monthly price × missed months`

For a standard R49 monthly Simple Slips subscription, recovery must not turn several missed R49 renewals into a larger automatic catch-up debit.

Any future automatic debit must correspond to the legitimate prospective subscription cycle.

If determining the correct next billing date is ambiguous:

* do not guess;
* do not charge;
* surface the account for manual review.

---

# 3. Existing duplicate Paystack subscriptions

For customers such as the confirmed Antebellum case, where multiple Paystack `SUB_*` relationships exist:

Do not:

* charge either subscription to recover historical periods;
* allow both subscriptions to continue intentionally as a way of recovering arrears;
* create another subscription;
* automatically select a subscription;
* automatically cancel a subscription as part of this task.

The account remains manual review until the canonical provider relationship is established.

Once resolved, billing should proceed prospectively through the canonical relationship.

Historical missed payments remain uncollected.

# 6. Management-link behaviour

The hosted management link exists to repair/update the payment method for the **existing canonical Paystack subscription**.

It must not be treated as a mechanism for collecting historical arrears.

Before returning a management link:

1. Resolve effective billing owner.
2. Acquire appropriate billing-owner protection.
3. Re-read trusted/recovered subscription identity.
4. Verify provider customer.
5. Verify provider plan.
6. Verify exact canonical `SUB_*`.
7. Confirm relationship is not ambiguous.
8. Only then request the Paystack management link.

If multiple viable provider subscriptions exist:

**no management link may be generated until the canonical relationship has been manually resolved.**

---

# 7. Response handling

Confirm the server:

* extracts only the hosted management URL from Paystack;
* validates that a usable URL was returned;
* returns a controlled error if the provider response is missing or malformed;
* does not silently fall back to recovery checkout;
* does not expose raw Paystack provider objects to the client;
* does not log the full management URL;
* does not initiate any historical collection.

If Paystack's actual response differs from the current implementation, fix only this integration boundary.

---

# 8. Customer-facing failure behaviour

If the management link cannot be generated:

* do not create another subscription;
* do not open generic checkout;
* do not charge an existing authorization;
* do not attempt to collect historical renewals;
* do not mark payment failed unless authoritative provider evidence proves an attempted payment failed;
* keep the customer in `payment_method_needs_attention`, `reconciling`, or equivalent safe state;
* show a controlled support/retry message.

Failure to generate a management link must not weaken duplicate-subscription protection.

---

# 9. Recovery checkout boundary

A new recovery checkout remains permissible only when the hardened recurring-readiness flow proves through complete provider inspection that:

* no viable existing Paystack subscription exists;
* there is no ambiguous provider relationship;
* there is no trusted/recoverable existing `SUB_*`.

Even then, the recovery checkout establishes billing **prospectively**.

It must not include historical missed subscription amounts.

Example:

If a R49/month customer missed three renewals because of the legacy billing problem, recovery must NOT create:

`R147 catch-up charge + current subscription`

or any equivalent automatic arrears collection.

Recovery should establish the legitimate prospective subscription relationship only.

* charge an `AUTH_*`;

* open checkout;

* calculate arrears and debit them;
* generate multiple future charges to compensate for missed historical periods.

Reconciliation may:

* classify recurring readiness;
* recover trusted identities where unambiguous;
* identify payment-method attention;
* identify ambiguity;
* identify accounts requiring manual review.

But it must not collect money.
