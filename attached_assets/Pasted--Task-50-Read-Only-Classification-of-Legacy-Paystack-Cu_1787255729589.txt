# 4. Classification buckets

Place each distinct billing owner into exactly ONE primary classification.

## A — HEALTHY_RECURRING

Requirements:

* exactly one canonical trusted/recoverable provider subscription;
* customer ownership matches;
* plan ownership matches;
* recurring authorization relationship is authoritative;
* `authorization.reusable === true`;
* no conflicting provider subscription exists;
* no unresolved billing ambiguity.

Recommended future action:

`NO ACTION`

---

## B — IDENTITY_RECOVERABLE

Requirements:

* local trusted `SUB_*` is missing or incomplete;
* complete provider inspection finds exactly ONE viable subscription;
* exact customer matches;
* exact plan matches;
* no competing viable provider subscription exists;
* ownership is unambiguous.

This does NOT authorize recovery during this task.

Recommended future action:

`SAFE IDENTITY RECOVERY CANDIDATE`

No mutation now.

---

## C — PAYMENT_METHOD_ATTENTION

Requirements:

* exactly one canonical provider subscription can be established;
* provider relationship belongs unambiguously to the billing owner and plan;
* recurring authorization/payment instrument is non-reusable, failing, or otherwise requires customer action;
* no competing viable provider subscription exists.

Recommended future action:

`CUSTOMER PAYMENT-METHOD RECOVERY`

Because:

`PAYSTACK_SUBSCRIPTION_MANAGEMENT_LINK_ENABLED=false`

do not generate a management link.

No checkout should be created merely because the payment method needs attention.

---

## D — MULTIPLE_PROVIDER_SUBSCRIPTIONS

Requirements:

* more than one viable/relevant Paystack subscription exists for the same billing owner/customer/plan;
* automatic canonical selection is unsafe.

This includes patterns like the confirmed Antebellum case.

Recommended future action:

`MANUAL PROVIDER REVIEW`

Do not:

* select newest;
* select oldest;
* select based on billing date;
* cancel either;
* recover either;
* create checkout.

---

## E — NO_PROVIDER_SUBSCRIPTION

Requirements:

Complete authoritative Paystack inspection proves:

* correct customer was inspected;
* relevant plan was inspected;
* pagination is complete;
* zero viable provider subscriptions exist.

Recommended future action:

`PROSPECTIVE RECOVERY CHECKOUT CANDIDATE`

Do NOT create checkout during this task.

---

## F — PROVIDER_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE

Use when:

* Paystack unavailable;
* provider timeout;
* pagination incomplete;
* provider response malformed;
* customer mapping cannot be proven;
* ownership unclear;
* provider evidence conflicts;
* required authorization evidence unavailable.

Recommended future action:

`REVIEW / RETRY LATER`

Do not guess.

---

## G — LOCAL_DATA_ANOMALY

Use when the Simple Slips data itself is contradictory enough that provider classification cannot safely resolve the account.

Examples:

* multiple local active subscriptions unexpectedly;
* conflicting billing owners;
* impossible plan relationships;
* duplicate historical relationships requiring investigation;
* local payment/subscription state contradicts trusted provider evidence in a way that requires engineering review.

Recommended future action:

`ENGINEERING REVIEW`

No mutation.
