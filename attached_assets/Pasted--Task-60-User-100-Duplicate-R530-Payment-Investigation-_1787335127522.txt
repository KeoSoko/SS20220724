# 5. Determine whether there are TWO provider transactions

This is the most important question.

Use existing locally stored provider transaction IDs/references first.

If the existing application has an approved **read-only Paystack verification/retrieval mechanism**, it may be used to verify specific known transaction references/IDs.

Do NOT perform broad customer mutation or subscription operations.

Determine:

### Scenario A

Two distinct successful Paystack transactions exist.

This indicates a real duplicate customer charge.

### Scenario B

One Paystack transaction exists but two local payment records exist.

This indicates local duplication, not necessarily a duplicate customer charge.

### Scenario C

One charge was successful and another failed/abandoned.

No duplicate successful collection.

### Scenario D

Evidence remains insufficient to prove provider-side duplication.

Manual/provider review required.

Do not infer Scenario A merely from `total_paid` or duplicate local rows.
