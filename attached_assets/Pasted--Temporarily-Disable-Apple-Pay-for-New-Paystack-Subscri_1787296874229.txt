# Temporarily Disable Apple Pay for New Paystack Subscription Checkout

We have identified a probable recurring-billing incompatibility/risk involving Apple Pay-created Paystack subscriptions.

Implement a temporary, fail-closed safety gate preventing Apple Pay from being used to establish **new Simple Slips recurring subscriptions through Paystack**.

This is a preventative change only.

DO NOT DEPLOY after implementation. Stop for review.

## Required behaviour

For new Simple Slips subscription checkout:

```text
Card → AVAILABLE
Apple Pay → UNAVAILABLE
```

This applies to:

* Premium Monthly
* Premium Annual
* Team subscription plans
* new subscriptions
* prospective recovery subscription checkout

Do not affect unrelated one-off payment functionality if any exists.

## Important implementation requirement

Do not merely hide an Apple Pay button in the Simple Slips frontend.

Trace how Paystack Checkout currently determines available payment channels and enforce the restriction at the Paystack checkout initialization/configuration layer wherever supported.

The objective is:

> Paystack must not allow Apple Pay to create a new recurring Simple Slips `SUB_*`.

If Paystack does not provide a supported way for the current integration to restrict Apple Pay while retaining Card, STOP and report the limitation rather than inventing an unsupported implementation.

## Existing customers

Do NOT:

* disable existing Paystack subscriptions;
* cancel subscriptions;
* alter existing Apple Pay-created subscriptions;
* change subscription identities;
* change recurring-readiness values;
* change next billing dates;
* remove paid access;
* refund transactions;
* collect missed payments;
* create replacement subscriptions;
* contact customers.

Existing Apple Pay-originated customers will be remediated separately.

## Existing billing protections

Preserve all existing safeguards, including:

* server-owned checkout attempts;
* server-generated Paystack references;
* billing-owner locking;
* immutable checkout terms;
* checkout idempotency;
* exact payment verification;
* provider subscription identity checks;
* `authorization.reusable === true` requirement;
* recurring-readiness states;
* duplicate subscription protection;
* management-link feature gate;
* stored-authorization charging disabled;
* prospective-only recovery policy;
* deferred webhook handling.

Do not weaken any of these protections.

## Customer experience

Do not expose a broken Apple Pay option and allow the customer to discover the restriction after attempting payment.

Where Simple Slips controls the payment-method presentation, Apple Pay should not be offered for subscription setup.

Normal Card subscription checkout should continue working exactly as before.

Do not add alarming customer messaging.

If messaging is necessary, use neutral wording such as:

`Apple Pay is temporarily unavailable for subscription payments. Please use a bank card.`

## Feature gate

Prefer a fail-closed server-owned configuration such as:

```text
PAYSTACK_APPLE_PAY_SUBSCRIPTIONS_ENABLED=false
```

Requirements:

* missing → disabled;
* false → disabled;
* only explicit `true` may enable it;
* server-side enforcement required;
* frontend state may mirror the server configuration but must not be the only protection.

Do not enable the flag in production.
