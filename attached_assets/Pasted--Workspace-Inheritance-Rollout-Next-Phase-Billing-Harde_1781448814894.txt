## Investigation + Implementation

Perform a complete audit and implementation plan for:

### 1. Authorization Persistence

Determine:

* Where Paystack authorization_code is currently captured
* Where customer_code is currently stored
* Whether existing subscribers can be backfilled

Implement:

* Persistent storage of authorization_code
* Persistent storage of customer_code
* Migration strategy for existing records

Goal:

Future support for one-click upgrades without checkout.

---

### 2. Webhook Plan Resolution

Audit every place where plan activation is determined.

Current concern:

Plan selection appears to rely partly on payment amount.

Examples:

Solo = R49
Team S = R245
Team M = R490
Team L = R980

Amount-based resolution will misclassify Team Plans.

Implement deterministic resolution using:

* Paystack plan code
  OR
* Explicit metadata

Never amount guessing.

---

### 3. Plan Mapping Architecture

Design and implement support for:

subscription_plans.max_seats

subscription_plans.paystack_plan_code

No Team Plans yet.

Only the infrastructure necessary to support them later.

---

### 4. Subscription Activation Flow

Audit:

* charge.success
* subscription.create
* renewals
* cancellations

Ensure:

* Plan activation is based on plan identity
* Not payment amount
* Not hardcoded plan IDs
